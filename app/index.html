Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages, built from the official trophy descriptions. Play the story first and keep a separate save before every named story mission — the nine missable trophies are the ones tied to a single named mission or a one-off choice. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the story ends. Where a place or step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so plainly: “to be confirmed at launch”.
